01 — About

Learning the craft behind the sound.

I first began playing music at 13, and what started as an interest quickly became an important part of how I express myself and understand the world around me. By the time I was 16, I had begun recording and producing my own music, which introduced me to the technical and creative possibilities of audio. Through that experience, I became increasingly interested in what happens behind the music—not only how a song is performed, but how recording, mixing, and production can shape the way a listener experiences it.

That curiosity ultimately led me to pursue Music & Sound Recording. I fell in love with audio engineering and with the process of learning how to mix a song so that it feels alive and captures the qualities that made me fall in love with music I heard on the radio as a child. I earned my A.S. degree in Music Industry from CT State Community College, where I developed a foundation in the music industry and continued exploring my interest in audio.

I am currently continuing my education at the University of New Haven, pursuing a B.A. in Music & Sound Recording. As I develop my technical and creative skills, I am excited to continue exploring recording, mixing, production, and the many ways sound can bring an artist's vision to life. My goal is to build on the foundation I've developed so far while continuing to learn, collaborate, and grow as an audio professional.

Project #2

"Lightning Doesn't Strike Twice

Lightning Doesn't Strike Twice represents a major step forward in my development as a producer and audio engineer. Written, recorded, mixed, and mastered entirely by me over a five-month period, the six-song EP was created during the summer when I began learning Logic Pro. I produced the entire project within Logic Pro while also experimenting with Splice's royalty-free sample library to expand the sounds and textures I could incorporate into my productions. Using samples allowed me to explore sounds and production techniques that would have otherwise been difficult to achieve with my level of studio experience at the time.
